ClipFarm

A personal clipping tool. It is not a public service and has no sign-ups.

What it does

  1. The owner sends a link to one of their own (or licensed) streams or videos to a private Telegram bot.
  2. ClipFarm finds the best moments and cuts them into short vertical clips with captions.
  3. The owner reviews every clip in Telegram and approves or skips each one by hand.
  4. Only approved clips are published, on a schedule, to the owner's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts through their official APIs.

Who uses it

Only its owner, on their own accounts. ClipFarm does not access, collect or post anything for other people.
